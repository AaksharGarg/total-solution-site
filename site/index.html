परिचय · About This Vision

What is Total Solution?

Total Solution is a self-published vision document written by Swati Garg, inspired by Sant Shri K.C. Ravi Ji. It lays out one straightforward idea: design cities so that farming, industry, homes and every essential service sit within a few kilometres of each other — so that 140 crore Indians can live well without stripping the country of the forests, farmland and clean air they depend on.

It is not a government plan or a policy paper — it is one citizen's working proposal, complete with maps, area calculations and a FAQ, offered for anyone to read, question and carry forward.

The document opens its first chapter by taking the word Bhagwaan apart. Each syllable is one of the five elements — which is why, in its argument, harming nature and harming God are the same act.

भ+ग+व+अ+न=भगवान
भभूमिEarth
गगगनSky
ववायुAir
अअग्निFire
ननीरWater

भाग 5 · The Proposal

A City You Never Have to Leave

The author draws the whole plan on two pages. A 15 × 15 km block where farmland and cities alternate around an untouched forest — and then, inside any one of those cities, a 5 × 5 km square laid out in mirrored bands around a pond. Both are redrawn below, to scale.

Because school, hospital, market and farm all sit inside a five-kilometre radius, there's barely any need for a personal vehicle — and the traffic, noise and pollution that come with one disappear along with it.

🏫 School within 2 km 🏥 Hospital within 5 km 🚶 Minimal private vehicles needed 🌳 Forest kept untouched at the core

लेखक के शब्दों में · In the Author's Words

A Few Lines From the Document

संतुलन ही सबसे बड़ी व्यवस्था है, यही देश की प्रगति है और खुशहाली भी। "Balance is the greatest system of all — that is both a nation's progress and its happiness."
हम पहले समस्या खड़ी करते हैं, फिर उसका समाधान ढूंढते हैं। व्यवस्था ऐसी होनी चाहिए कि समस्या ही न हो। "We create the problem first, then hunt for its solution. The system should be built so the problem never arises."
प्रकृति के लिए इंसान उतना ही तुच्छ है, जितना इंसान के लिए दूसरे प्राणी। "To nature, a human being matters exactly as much as every other creature matters to us."
